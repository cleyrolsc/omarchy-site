<p>Omarchy ships with a minimal <a href="https://starship.rs/">Starship</a> prompt by default. That’s how I like to keep my prompt. I don’t need to know the user, because it’s always me, and I don’t need to know the time, because it’s always at the top.</p>

<p><img src="/manual/images/prompt.webp" alt="prompt" /></p>

<p>If you want more information or style, you can change the <a href="https://starship.rs/">Starship.rs</a> configuration in <code>~/.config/starship.toml</code>. There’s a lot you can do. Just don’t go overboard (or do go overboard, do whatever you want, it’s your computer!)</p>
